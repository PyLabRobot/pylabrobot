pylabrobot.brooks.precise_flex.PreciseFlexArm#

class pylabrobot.brooks.precise_flex.PreciseFlexArm(driver: PreciseFlexDriver)#

Bases: object

The arm of a PreciseFlex: its joints, read and moved through the controller.

Reached as driver.arm. Sends through the driver’s send_command; every move whose target joint states are known before it is sent goes through _guarded_move.
