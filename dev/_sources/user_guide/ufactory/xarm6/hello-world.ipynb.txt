    "### Joint movement\n",
    "\n",
    "Move in joint space using the {class}`~pylabrobot.ufactory.xarm6.joints.XArm6Axis` enum. `speed` is in deg/s and `mvacc` in deg/s^2. Axes you omit keep their current angle.\n",
    "\n",
    "```{warning}\n",
    "Moving to arbitrary joint positions can cause the arm to collide with its base, the gripper, or nearby equipment. Verify coordinates carefully in a safe workspace first.\n",
